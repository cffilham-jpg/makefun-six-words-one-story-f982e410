A complete story in exactly six words, written by `main.py` (Python 3.13,
standard library only) to `entry.txt`:

> Sold the house. Kept the ghost.

A whole life is implied in two short sentences: a house sold, a loss
endured, and the one thing that could not be let go. The turn lands in
the second sentence, and what the ghost is — memory, grief, or something
else — is left for the reader to fill in.
